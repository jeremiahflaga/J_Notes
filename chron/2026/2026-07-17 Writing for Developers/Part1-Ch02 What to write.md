# Chapter 2: What to write


## Prioritizing ideas: The 3 Ps

If you want to earn a
spot in the world of respected engineering blog posts, it’s absolutely nonnegotiable that
you choose a topic that’s **rooted in your own experiences** — something that you can provide
a uniquely interesting perspective on.

Does your topic meet at least one of the **“3 Ps”**? Is it about
something that you’re particularly **proud of, pained by, or passionate about**?
