= Chapter 8: Coming to Terms with an Author

== Quotes

RULE 5: Find the important words and through them come to terms with the author.


== Recall / Retrieval 

p 96ff

It is not enough to know or read the "words" or phrases the author uses. One has to understand what the author means when he uses those words/phrases. Because one word/phrase can have multiple meanings or terms. And two different words/phrases can have the same meaning/term.

p 102

Words that you find hard to understand might be some of the keywords the author uses.
