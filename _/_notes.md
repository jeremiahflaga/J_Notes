Reading history are in the metadata part of each adoc file, with this formatting:

. Sample history indicating completion of whole book (or even when only those sections deemed important are completed)
[source,asciidoc]
----
:v-1.0.0: 2019-08-11 | First reading
:v-2.0.0: 2019-12-22 | Second reading
:v-3.0.0: 2020-04-19 | Third reading
:v-4.0.0: 2021-04-17 | Fourth reading
----

. Sample history indicating goal was not attained on first reading
[source,asciidoc]
----
:v-1.0.0: 2019-11-02 | First reading (finished part 1 only)
:v-1.1.0: 2025-09-20 | Second reading (planning to read parts 1 & 2 only)
:v-2.0.0: 3000-01-01 | Third reading
----
