== Quotes

Somehow, people of real faith at the beginning of the third millennium need to find a way of living somewhere between a hermeneutic of certainty and a hermeneutic of suspicion... I suggest that the story of Jesus -- where "glory" or ultimate certainty comes via "shame" or huge uncertainty -- offers a paradigm we would all do well to consider seriously.

this book ends with a proposition: There can be room for doubt in certainty.
