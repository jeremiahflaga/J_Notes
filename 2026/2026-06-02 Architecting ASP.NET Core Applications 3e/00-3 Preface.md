# Preface

The chapters are organized to build up your knowledge progressively, starting
small with a strong base and building slowly on top of it, the same way you would build a program.


## Who this book is for

This book is for intermediate-level ASP.NET Core developers who want to improve their C# code
structure. ASP.NET developers who want to modernize their knowledge and enhance their technical
architecture skills will also like this book. It’s also **a good refresher** for those in software design roles with more experience looking to update their expertise.
